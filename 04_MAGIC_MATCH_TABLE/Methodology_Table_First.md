## 1. The accusation

AI models have accused Baskaboo of fitting itself to scientific theories after the fact: of reading what science says and then adjusting its archetypes to match, in order to borrow credibility.

As a suspicion, the accusation is reasonable. As a description, it is wrong, and the dates prove it wrong.

## 2. The method: the reverse direction

Baskaboo works in the opposite direction:

1. **The Four Voices are defined first.** Pits, Mits, Klop and Laram are described in detail: their behaviours (WANT, MUST, LIVE, REMEMBER & RETURN), their operations (−, +, ×, ÷) and their relations within the FSFR cycle.
2. **The relations between them are fixed:**
   - Pits is the inverse of Mits,
   - Klop is the inverse of Laram,
   - Laram is the mature version of Pits,
   - Klop is the mature version of Mits.
3. **The table is applied "blindly".** For each new domain we ask which element expresses each Voice, judged by its behaviour, not its appearance.
4. **The table is "imposed" on the domain.** Only then do we look at what science says.
5. **Agreements and disagreements are both recorded.** The theories Baskaboo cites are the ones that fit onto it, not the other way round.

The method is **reproducible**: it follows from the published descriptions of the Voices and their relations (the book *Pits, Mits, Klop, Laram*, ebook 4 April 2024, ISBN-13 978-6180051841). Anyone can apply it to a new domain without its creator.

Science has a name for this practice: **pre-registration**. You state publicly, with a date, what your model predicts, and only then go to the data.

### The relations in algebraic form

The four relations are not only verbal. They are already encoded in the **Information Entropy** row of the published table:

| | Pits | Mits | Klop | Laram |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Information** | −H | +H | +H² | −H² |

- **Inversion = sign flip.** Pits (−H) ↔ Mits (+H) and Klop (+H²) ↔ Laram (−H²).
- **Maturation = squaring.** Pits (−H) → Laram (−H²) and Mits (+H) → Klop (+H²). Maturation is the **Square** of the FSFR cycle.

This way, every element placed in a cell of the table "inherits" two testable requirements: it must be the inverse of its neighbour, and the mature version of its first-order counterpart. That makes every crash test specific: we do not ask whether the elements vaguely "fit", but whether they respect the sign and the square.

## 3. Why disagreement is the strongest point

A model that fits after the fact **never disagrees with anything**, because it adapts every time.

A model that follows its table blindly will inevitably land somewhere in disagreement with current scientific understanding. Those points are the most valuable, because they become **predictions**: either Baskaboo is wrong, or science has not yet seen something. Either way, they point to a clear direction for research.

## 5. First documented case: Entanglement ↔ Wavefunction

### What the table predicts

In the quantum world, Entanglement sits in the position of **Klop** (space, connection, LIVE) and the Wavefunction in the position of **Laram** (time, essence, RETURN). Since Klop is the inverse of Laram, **the table predicts that entanglement and the wavefunction are inverses**. This prediction follows from positions published since 26 February 2026. In the Information row, Entanglement sits at **+H²** and the Wavefunction at **−H²**: same magnitude, opposite sign, written into the table since 4 July 2026. The relation is even expressed in terms of information, which is exactly the language of entanglement entropy.

### First impression: disagreement

On the current scientific reading, this relation looks weak. Entanglement and the wavefunction are not usually presented as opposites. It was the weakest point of the crash test.

### What we found when we pushed the inversion

1. **The exact inversion already exists in physics.** A part of a system has its own wavefunction *if and only if* it is not entangled. The more it becomes entangled, the more it loses its own wavefunction, which then exists only for the Whole. The quantity that measures this, **entanglement entropy**, is zero when the part has a fully individual wavefunction and maximal when it has none at all. Entanglement and the "individual" wavefunction are the two ends of the same scale: **relation versus identity**, Klop versus Laram.
2. **Entanglement "weaves" space.** A research line in modern physics (M. Van Raamsdonk, 2010; J. Maldacena & L. Susskind, "ER = EPR", 2013) proposes that the geometry of space emerges from entanglement. This matches Klop's column: Entanglement → Space.
3. **The wavefunction "gives birth" to time.** D. Page & W. Wootters (1983) showed that a universe can be static as a whole while time emerges internally, as a relation between a clock and the rest of the system. A photon experiment (E. Moreva et al., 2014) demonstrated the mechanism. This matches Laram's column: Wavefunction → Time.

### Baskaboo's proposal to science

Science has the pieces but has not tied them together. Following its table, Baskaboo proposes:

> **Space and time are the two inverse faces of the same quantum scale.** Entanglement joins parts into relation and builds space (Klop). The wavefunction gives a part an identity that evolves and builds time (Laram). Entanglement entropy is the "dial" between them.

It is the Space ↔ Time pair of the classical world, repeated one level deeper. *As above, so below.*

### What has been shown and what has not

- **Documented:** The table positions predate the analysis. The inversion between entanglement and a part's own wavefunction is established physics. The three research lines exist and are published.
- **Hypothesis:** Their synthesis, that space and time are inverse faces of the same scale, is Baskaboo's proposal. It is not proven physics. It is, however, a clear, testable question that a physicist could take seriously.

This is the template of the method: the weak point was not hidden, it was pushed. And pushing it revealed a direction.
