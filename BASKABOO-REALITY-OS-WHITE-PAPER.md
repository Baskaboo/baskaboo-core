## 2. LOGICAL GAPS AND CLARIFICATIONS

**Response to Clarification 1 (Howard / φ)**

- **[Established]** The forward direction is well defined: in φ² = φ + 1, replace φ by 1 everywhere φ appears. The "1" on the right is already the Level A unit. The result is 1² = 1 + 1. The left side evaluates to 1 and the right side to 2, so the formal result is "1 = 2", and the paper's "1·1 = 2" reads the right-hand value as the value of the left.
- **[Established]** The reverse direction is not unique. Replacing every 1 in 1² = 1 + 1 by φ gives φ² = 2φ. Replacing only the first two gives φ² = φ + 1.
- **[Interpretive]** The ambiguity is informative: φ² = φ + 1 is itself a bridge equation containing both units, φ (Level B) in the multiplicative relation and 1 (Level A) in the additive term. A rule is needed: the "+1" is the Level A unit and stays; the other two become φ.
